# Personal Career Notes & Profile
Author: Candidate Profile
Target Decision: IBPS PO (Banking) vs Software Development Career

## 1. Candidate Background & Education
- Degree: B.Tech in Computer Science (Graduated with 7.8 CGPA).
- Core Strengths: Strong fundamentals in Python, SQL, and database concepts. Good at building projects and problem solving.
- Weaknesses: Slower at competitive speed-math tricks under extreme exam time pressure (e.g., 60 seconds per 5 questions in banking prelims).
- Current Savings & Runway: 6 to 9 months of living expenses saved. Needs financial independence without a 2-3 year gap.

## 2. Option A: IBPS Probationary Officer (Banking Sector)
- Entry Process: 3 Stages — Preliminary Exam (Speed Math, Reasoning, English), Mains Exam (Data Analysis, GA, Banking Awareness, Descriptive Writing), followed by Face-to-Face Interview.
- Annual Vacancies: ~3,000 to 5,000 seats across all participating public sector banks.
- Exam Competition: Over 800,000 to 1,000,000 applicants compete annually (Success rate < 0.5%).
- Starting Salary: Approx Rs 55,000 to Rs 65,000/month (6.5 to 7.5 LPA gross CTC including perks & allowances).
- Pros: Extreme job security, public prestige, fixed promotions via internal exams, standard banking holidays.
- Cons: Frequent rural/semi-urban transfers every 3-4 years, commercial branch sales targets, slower wage growth compared to private tech.

## 3. Option B: Software Development Engineer (Tech Industry)
- Entry Process: Resume shortlisting, Coding round (DSA & Problem Solving in Python/Java/C++), Technical System Design & Project Interview, HR/Behavioral round.
- Starting Salary: Ranges widely from 5 LPA (service firms) to 12-20 LPA (product startups / tech MNCs). Average ~7.5 LPA.
- Growth Ceiling: High performers double their compensation in 3-5 years through performance bonuses and strategic company switches.
- Pros: High financial upside, merit-driven career progression, flexible or remote work options, continuous skill appreciation.
- Cons: Market cyclicity (layoffs during recessions), continuous upskilling required, higher everyday deliverables pressure.

## 4. Personal Preferences & Long-Term Goals
- Preferred Location: Major tech hubs (Bangalore, Pune, Hyderabad, or NCR) or remote. Prefers not to relocate to rural branches.
- Risk Tolerance: Moderate. Willing to take performance risk for higher financial growth.
- Ideal 5-Year Goal: Reach a leadership role with a package exceeding 20 LPA while retaining work-life balance.
